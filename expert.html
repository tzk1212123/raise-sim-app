Outcome model

  • Digitised from the RAISE paper (Fig. 7a, 8d, S4a, Tables 1 & S2); raw data is not public.
  • Model A (physics): Rubingh mixed micelles → competitive adsorption → wetting tension → angle via a calibrated adhesion-tension line. Structure check: recovers SDS 99% CMC 8.9 mM (literature 8.2).
  • Model B: Gaussian-process emulator on the digitised surface (independent cross-check).
  • Surface family: posterior draws of model A, so conclusions must hold across plausible worlds.

Fairness rules

  • Same task, grid, budget and identical initial random experiments for every planner.
  • Planners see only public records; failed detections are missing values, never numbers.
  • Each planner recommends one formulation, which is validated on a fresh rig.
  • LLM ablations: blind (no chemistry names) and permuted (feedback scrambled).
